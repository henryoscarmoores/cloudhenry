✈️ What we provide

We find cheap flights and email them to you, and members can search every fare we hold. We are not a travel agent, tour operator or package organiser, and we do not sell flights, hotels or holidays. Any booking you make is a separate contract between you and the airline or agent. We are not part of it, and we cannot guarantee a fare is still available or still that price once you click through. Always check the price on the airline's own site before you pay.

💳 Membership, trial and billing

What it costs. Membership is £2.99 a month or £29 a year. Every membership starts with a 40 day free trial.

The trial. Card details are taken when the trial starts. Nothing is charged during the 40 days. We email you a reminder before the trial ends, telling you the date the first payment will be taken and how to cancel. If you do nothing, the membership continues and the first payment is taken on that date.

Renewals. After the trial, membership renews automatically, monthly or yearly depending on the plan you chose, until you cancel. We email a receipt for every payment.

Cancelling. You can cancel at any time, in two clicks, from your account page at My CloudHenry, or by emailing us. There is no phone call, no notice period and no cancellation fee. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Cooling off. New members in the UK have 14 days to change their mind and get a refund under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. Tell us within 14 days of joining and we will refund you.

🔒 Privacy and your data

CloudHenry Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described here.

What we collect and why

  • Your email address, and your name if you give it, so we can send the emails you signed up for. Our basis for this is your consent, and for members, performing our contract with you.
  • Your chosen airport, so the right weekly email reaches you.
  • Whether you opened or clicked an email, so we know which deals are worth sending. You can turn this off by reading in plain text.
  • Payment details, if you become a member. These are handled by Stripe and we never see or store your card number.
  • Basic analytics about pages viewed, and where you arrived from, so we know which posts bring people in. This is aggregated and not used to identify you.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of suppliers to run the service, and they only ever process your data on our instructions: Ghost (the site and member accounts), Mailgun (sending our emails), Stripe (payments), and Cloudflare (the sign-up form and site delivery). We do not sell your data, and we never share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it. While you are on our list or a member, and for 24 months after you unsubscribe or cancel, after which we delete it. Records we must keep for tax purposes are kept for six years.

Your rights. You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Email henryswalk@gmail.com and we will deal with it within one month, free of charge. Every email we send has a one click unsubscribe link, and you can also manage this from your account page.

If you are unhappy. Tell us first and we will try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
